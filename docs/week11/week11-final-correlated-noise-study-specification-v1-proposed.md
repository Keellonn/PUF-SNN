### Model and fixed reconstruction/protocol profile
For oscillator i, reading r, device d and trial t in one condition,
```text
f(d,t,i,r) = 100 + M(d,i) + C(d,t)
             + sigma*sqrt(rho)*Z(d,t,i)
             + sigma*sqrt(1-rho)*E(d,t,i,r)
M(d,i) ~ N(0,1), once per device
Z and E ~ N(0,1), separate indexed domains
```

Z is oscillator-specific and shared across readings of that attempt; E is independent across readings/oscillators, with independent oscillator components of Z and separate manufacturing streams. Between distinct condition domains/trials, use distinct indexed measurement streams. Manufacturing persists across all cells. No aging, added environmental offset, shared group manufacturing term, clipping or changed simulator is proposed. Sigma and C have abstract additive frequency units, not measured Hz.

Define the measurement-noise residual `eta(i,r) = f(d,t,i,r) - 100 - M(d,i) - C(d,t)`. Conditional on the device's fixed manufacturing characteristics M and the controlled common-mode offset C, `Var[eta(i,r) | M,C] = sigma²` and, for distinct readings r,s of the same attempt, `Cov[eta(i,r),eta(i,s) | M,C] = rho sigma²`. For distinct paired oscillators i,j, the residual differential has conditional variance `2 sigma²` and cross-reading conditional covariance `2 rho sigma²`. These measurement-noise moments hold across rho and exclude manufacturing variation. Across newly generated devices with manufacturing SD=1, the complete frequency differential instead has variance `2 + 2 sigma²` and same-attempt cross-reading covariance `2 + 2 rho sigma²`; the common C still cancels. Thus rho0 means independent measurement disturbances conditional on the device, not independent repeated outcomes after averaging over persistent device margins. Analog residual rho is not binary-error correlation. The correlation model itself is unchanged.

Retain 128 oscillators in adjacent pairs, 64 response bits with positions 0..62 used by BCH(63,36,t=5), a 32-bit credential plus four fixed padding bits, independent credential verifier, admission service, Authentication-v2 and a stationary 120-sample sequence-zero window with recording callbacks. Retain the reviewed protocol profile and explicit timeout/session/window/capacity settings from the inherited configuration in the future final config; changes require prospective review. A 256-bit derived session key does not raise credential entropy above 32 bits.

S performs one actual reading and one reconstruction/decode. M performs three actual readings, one vote and one reconstruction/decode. S and M share device, enrollment, credential, Z and indexed reading zero within a block; M additionally reads indices 1 and 2. Separate reader objects perform actual matched acquisitions. T retrieves the same enrolled credential from trusted memory, provisioned before timing, with zero readings/votes/decodes and no fallback. Each arm gets fresh sender, receiver/verifier session, admission and consumer state, with the same enrollment/verifier material. OS-random keys, nonces, session IDs and capabilities remain unmatched nuisance variation, never deterministic protocol entropy.

At rho1, all three PUF readings agree within an attempt, and matched S/M C/D/I/W must agree exactly. Delta(1)=0 is a model/reconstruction identity, not a claim of zero FRR. The common control must preserve bits and C/D/I/W relative to `op_rho000`. A floating-point common-offset ordering change is a numerical/infrastructure fault, not a scientific common-mode failure. ## Statistical analysis and denominator contract

For PUF arm p in {S,M}, every scheduled, completed reconstruction has exactly one outcome: C=correct enrolled credential, D=decoder failure, I=invalid format/padding, W=valid-format wrong credential. For complete data in condition c:

```text
N_p,c = C_p,c + D_p,c + I_p,c + W_p,c = 6,000
FRR_p,c = (D_p,c + I_p,c + W_p,c) / N_p,c = 1 - C_p,c/N_p,c
Delta(rho) = FRR_S(op_rho) - FRR_M(op_rho)
A(rho) = Delta(0) - Delta(rho), at sigma = 0.15 throughout
Principal contrast: A(0.90)
```

Positive Delta means voting benefit; positive A means attenuation. Retain signed estimates and intervals without clipping negative values. The reference for every A is the **fresh final-study `op_rho000`**, never the calibration estimate or the sigma .10 nominal cell. A(0)=0 by definition. A(1)=Delta(0) under the rho1 identity. Report the full four-point Delta curve, A(.50), principal A(.90), and the rho1 boundary. Report separate S/M absolute FRRs and nominal/common controls. Four rho points do not establish monotonicity or a continuous response curve.

For trial failure indicators F=1 for D/I/W and 0 for C, form each device's 50-trial mean failure rate and benefit `b_d,c = mean_t(F_S,d,t,c - F_M,d,t,c)`. Estimate Delta by the equal mean of the 120 device benefits; estimate A by the equal mean of paired within-device `b_d,op_rho000 - b_d,op_rho`. Balanced trials make this equivalent to pooled complete matched counts. Publish both beneficial (S fails/M correct) and harmful (S correct/M fails) discordances; their net count divided by 6,000 equals Delta. No pooling over S/M/T or across controls is allowed.

### BER, error histograms and secondary uncertainty
All BER and error-count summaries apply separately to each condition and PUF arm, relative to that device's fixed noiseless enrollment response. Use exactly response-bit positions J={0,...,62}; position 63 of the 64-bit response is excluded. Let `e_d,t,r = sum_{j in J} 1[reading_d,t,r[j] != reference_d[j]]` and let `e_eff_d,t` be the corresponding error count for the effective response supplied to reconstruction. A complete condition/arm has N=6,000 attempts and K=1 actual reading per S attempt or K=3 per M attempt.

- **Raw BER:** `sum_d,t,r(e_d,t,r) / (63 * R)`, where R is the actual number of readings, R=K*N in complete data (6,000 for S; 18,000 for M). The observation unit is an actual reading. S's acquisition and M's matched first acquisition both remain actual operations in their separate arm summaries; they are not pooled as independent observations.
- **First-reading BER:** `sum_d,t(e_d,t,0) / (63 * N)`, one indexed reading-zero response per attempt. Matched S/M first-reading counts must agree.
- **Effective-response BER:** `sum_d,t(e_eff_d,t) / (63 * N)`, one response per attempt: reading zero for S or the bitwise Majority-3 vote for M. For S, all three BER definitions coincide in complete data. BER is measured before BCH correction and does not replace credential-reconstruction FRR.
- **Error histograms:** for each integer h=0..63, raw H_raw(h) counts actual readings with h selected-bit errors and sums to R; first H_first(h) counts reading-zero responses and sums to N; effective H_eff(h) counts reconstruction-input responses and sums to N. Report integer bin counts and relative frequencies H_raw(h)/R, H_first(h)/N and H_eff(h)/N. Do not use bits or three-reading sets as histogram observation units. Keep each condition/arm separate, including the linked common control.

Incomplete-run diagnostics may show observed reading/response counts and partial histograms with explicit denominators and unknown/missing work; they do not acquire complete-study inference eligibility.
