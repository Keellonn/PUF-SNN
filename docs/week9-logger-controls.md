# Laptop logger control checks

October 28, 2026. Start, Skip, Stop and Continue are connected in the separate
LoggerControlsLaptop scene. Two manual Play Mode visits reconcile in Python.
Recording and automatic start are disabled in the saved scene. The exact tested
scope and remaining checks are in the [evidence bundle](../results/week-9/keegan/logger-controls-20261028-001/README.md).

skips before/during recording, Continue into block two and operator Stop during
preparation and active recording. Both filesets have complete trial accounting
and source diagnostics. Quality refusal without valid XR tracking is expected.
This is not proof of physical tracking or rendering performance.
